The tea cafe culture

Tea in India is more than a beverage. It is an emotion, a ritual and a reason to connect.

Tea Post was founded to elevate the humble chai tapri into a clean, modern and comfortable cafe, without losing the warmth of traditional flavour.

At Tea Post Vapi we serve freshly prepared tea made with quality ingredients, alongside traditional Gujarati snacks and global favourites.

Whether you are starting your morning, catching up with friends or taking a much-needed work break, our outlet is made for your daily refreshment.

A cup, step by step

The idea behind every glass we pour.

  1. Boil

    Boil water with ginger and elaichi until the kitchen smells of it.

  2. Brew

    Add tea leaves and milk, then let it simmer slowly.

  3. Pour

    Strain, pour hot and serve in a glass or a clay kullad.
