The Tea Cafe Culture

Tea in India is more than a beverage. It is an emotion, a ritual, and a reason to connect.

Tea Post was founded to elevate the humble chai tapri into a clean, modern and comfortable cafe, without losing the warmth of traditional flavor.

At Tea Post Vapi, we serve freshly prepared tea made with quality ingredients, alongside traditional Gujarati snacks and global favorites. Whether you're starting your morning, catching up with friends or taking a much-needed work break, our outlet is made for your daily refreshment.

  • Freshly brewedQuality ingredients in every cup.
  • Hygiene firstA clean, vibrant and welcoming space.
  • Open to everyoneStudents, families and professionals are all at home here.
